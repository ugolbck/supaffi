# Roadmap

What's coming to Supaffi, in the order we plan to build it. There are no dates.
What already shipped is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md), and what Supaffi
handles today is in [What Supaffi handles](what-supaffi-handles.md).

Have a request? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ugolbck/supaffi/issues).

## Just shipped

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Referrals keep their program's terms** | Move an affiliate to another program and the customers they already brought keep earning on the old program's terms. |
| **Forms that work with password managers** | Your password manager saves new passwords to the right account, on both the owner and affiliate side. |
| **Polar** | Sell through Polar, a merchant of record that handles your sales tax. Pick it when you add a product. Promo codes work there too. |
| **Email notifications** | Connect your email provider and hear when someone joins or sells. Affiliates hear when they earn and are paid, and reset their own password. |
| **Promo codes** | Give a program a discount and every affiliate claims their own code for it in one click. A customer using it credits them, even without clicking their link. |
| **Stripe Payment Links, buy buttons and pricing tables** | Sales through them earn commissions. The tracking script is all you add. |

## Up next

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Payouts** | A minimum payout per program, the reason each commission is on hold, a method, reference and note on every payout, and one file to pay everyone through PayPal or Wise. |
| **Import from Rewardful, Tolt, FirstPromoter and Affonso** | Upload an export, check the preview, and bring your affiliates, their links and their history. Nobody's link breaks. |
| **Paddle** | Sell through Paddle, another merchant of record. |

## Planned

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Commissions after fees** | An option per program to pay commissions on the sale minus the payment provider's fee. |
| **Applications and sales review** | Approve new affiliates before they go live, and hold sales that look like fraud until you review them. |
| **Claude and ChatGPT connection** | Ask about your program and manage it from your AI assistant, through MCP. |
| **Fixed and tiered rewards** | A flat amount per sale or signup, and a higher rate past a sales volume. |

## Later

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Tax forms** | Affiliates upload their tax form in their portal, and the Payouts page shows who is missing one. |
| **Lemon Squeezy** | Sell through Lemon Squeezy. |
| **Discount for the referred customer** | Customers who come through an affiliate get a discount too. |
| **Affiliate kit** | Your logo and colours on the affiliate pages, plus banners and copy ready to share. |
| **Alerts and webhooks** | A Slack message or a Zapier webhook when an affiliate signs up, a sale earns or a commission is flagged. |
| **Stats per link** | See which of an affiliate's links and sources bring sales. |
| **Late refunds** | Optionally take a refund that arrives after payout off the affiliate's next payout. |
| **Stripe problems in the dashboard** | See a disabled webhook or failing events without reading server logs. |

Contributors: the detailed version, with technical notes and known gaps, is in
